# what I'm thinking about right [now](https://sive.rs/now)
`last updated: 2026-06-07`

## building this website
After years of procrastinating on building a website, letting perfectionism get the best of me, I've finally decided to bite the bullet and start. I have no training in web dev, and websites seem to use all sorts of interacting frameworks and complicated structures, so there's definitely a bit of a learning curve. Luckily [@alshedivat](https://github.com/alshedivat/) has made this beautiful template for me, and I've been learning about web dev through exploring its little quirks and customizations. 

## training to hike mount rainier
The moment I laid eyes on that snowy peak, I knew I needed to find my way to the top some how. After over a year of learning what I didn't know I didn't know, I finally feel like I am ready to summit Mount Rainier. Finding a competent team that is available during a good weather window has been difficult, but I'm confident I can make my dream come true.

## (re) applying to grad school
Last Fall I applied to three PhD programs, all pretty competitive. I had low expectations, and the results were true to form. The first round was definitely a bit of a shot in the dark, but I figured I would learn from the experience - and I did. This year, I hope to go into it with a clearer direction and a better understanding of the system I am navigating. I am interested in continuing a research agenda focused on decision making in intelligent agents. Specifically, I want to explore how learning from past experience affects decision making, as well as how individually varying factors such as propensity for risk taking or exploration can affect decision making.
