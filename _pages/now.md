# what I'm thinking about right [now](https://nownownow.com/about)
`last updated: 2026-11-05`

## building this website
After years of procrastinating on building a website, letting perfectionism get the best of me, I've finally decided to bite the bullet and start. I have no training in web dev, and websites seem to use all sorts of interacting frameworks and complicated structures, so there's definitely a bit of a learning curve. Luckily [@alshedivat](https://github.com/alshedivat/) has made this beautiful template for me, and I've been learning about web dev through exploring its little quirks and customizations. I'm also working on a seperate, more personal website for my artistic endeavors and personal projects that I plan to build from scratch, hopefully taking the little things I've learned from putting this website together to create something actually unique and original.

## (re) applying to grad school
Last Fall I applied to three PhD programs, all pretty competitive. I had low expectations, and the results were true to form. The first round was definitely a bit of a shot in the dark, but I figured I would learn from the experience - and I did. This year, I hope to go into it with a clearer direction and a better understanding of the system I am navigating. I am interested in continuing a research agenda focused on understanding learning, mental representation, and decision making in intelligent agents. Specifically, I want to explore how individuals can utilize previously learned representations to quickly and flexibly learn new things, as well as how individually varying factors such as propensity for risk taking or exploration can affect decision making.

## american southwest tour/road trip
In 2024, as part of my move from New Jersey to Seattle, I completed a 10 day cross country road trip. It was extremely fun and I was able to explore many parts of the country I hadn't been able to explore before! This year, as I move back from Seattle to New Jersey to take some time for myself and work on personal projects, I am doing a much bigger road trip in the reverse direction, traversing south through California before heading west across the country and back up to the East Coast. My goal is to see much of the American wilderness I haven't seen before, visit many friends in far places, and learn more about the culture and way of living of people across the country while learning more about where my innate desires take me as I spend a lot of time with myself.
